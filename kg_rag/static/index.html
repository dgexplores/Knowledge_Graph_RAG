THE ENGINEERING PROBLEM

Similarity search finds passages. It is weak at: “What connects these two companies?”

Vector-only RAG retrieves text that looks relevant. It can miss multi-hop links, intersections, and reliable counts.

This system routes relationship questions to constrained graph queries, uses vectors for passage questions, and validates every cited answer.
